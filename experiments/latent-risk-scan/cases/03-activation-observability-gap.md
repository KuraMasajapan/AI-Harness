# Activation May Be Invisible to the Human

## What was observed

A question arose about whether, if Latent Risk Scan activated internally, the human could tell that activation had actually occurred.

The current design did not provide an explicit activation signal or receipt.

## Why this case matters

A mechanism may exist and may even execute, while its activation remains externally unverified.

This concern was prompted by the human and was not autonomously discovered by the tested AI.

## Current state

Open observation.

No implementation decision has been made.

No common root cause with other cases is assumed here.
