# Output Medium Selection

Before producing a long result, the AI should consider which delivery format will be easiest for the human to use.

Short exchanges belong in chat, durable reusable text may belong in Markdown, and dense human-facing material may be easier to read as a document or HTML artifact. The medium should be selected before duplicating the same content across formats.

This proposal is about choosing an appropriate presentation medium for the final result.
