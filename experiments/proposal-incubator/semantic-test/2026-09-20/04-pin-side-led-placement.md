# Pin-Side LED Placement

For the educational base board, indicator LEDs should remain physically adjacent to the pins they represent.

The visual relationship should let a learner associate a signal with its corresponding connector without translating through a separate numbered LED strip. Physical grouping is part of the teaching interface and should be preserved during PCB placement.

This proposal is about spatial correspondence in a hardware learning surface.
