# Proposal Synthesis

Several individually weak proposals may later reveal that they are fragments of one larger system.

The AI should be allowed to notice that different problems share a deeper structure and propose a single higher-level mechanism when one design could solve several of them at once.

This proposal is about creating system ideas from accumulated fragments rather than counting duplicates.
