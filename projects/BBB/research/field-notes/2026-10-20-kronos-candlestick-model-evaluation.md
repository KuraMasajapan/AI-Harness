# Kronos Financial Candlestick Foundation Model — fact check / BBB assessment

## Source and provenance
- X post by Roan (@RohOnChain), posted 2026-10-19 16:27 UTC:
  https://x.com/RohOnChain/status/2112218991414710384
- Official upstream repo:
  https://github.com/shiyu-coder/Kronos
- Original research: Shi et al., *Kronos: A Foundation Model for the Language of Financial Markets*, AAAI 2026, https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v40i30.39730 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.02739
- Released small model: https://huggingface.co/NeoQuasar/Kronos-small
- Independent counterevidence, **preprint** posted May 2026: Zhong et al., *Three-Year Evidence from Approximately 5,000 A-Share Stocks on the Limits of Pre-trained TSFM Integration*, https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6849321

## Factual checks
1. Kronos is a real open-source (MIT) autoregressive candlestick/time-series model. The paper reports training on **over 12 billion K-line records from 45 exchanges**. This is an author/paper report of pretraining scale, not independent BBB reproduction.
2. Models released publicly include `Kronos-mini` (~4.1M parameters, 2048 context), `Kronos-small` (~24.7M, 512 context) and `Kronos-base` (~102.3M, 512 context). Large model is not publicly released.
3. Official predictor supports CPU device fallback in `model/kronos.py`; practical prediction speed/RAM at specific hardware and 100 samples have **not** been measured.
4. Context 512 and next 24 candles are configurable example settings, not evidence of a profitable trading horizon or optimal choice.
5. **Material correction to X post:** X describes `sample_count=100` as producing 100 visible future paths whose spread is a risk measure. Official `auto_regressive_inference` in `model/kronos.py` repeats the input and generates sampled paths but then calls `np.mean(preds, axis=1)` and returns only their **mean**. Standard `predict()` does **not** return the path distribution. A separate experimental interface would be needed for individual samples, uncertainty calibration and distribution evaluation.
6. Authors report benchmark improvements in RankIC/volatility/synthetic-series metrics, **not verified live BTC/JPY risk-adjusted net returns**. Prediction quality does not automatically establish executable market edge.
7. Independent 2026 preprint on Chinese A-share *daily volatility*, not BTC/JPY return trading, reports that Kronos underperformed simple HAR-RV/GARCH benchmarks in its particular task. This bounds confidence; it does not prove Kronos fails in crypto.
8. Post's `accurately predicts next move`, `works on any normal laptop`, `100 futures show risk directly`, and zero-cost real trading claims are **not established** by the cited paper or code. Free model weights != free computation, data integration, or profitable trading.

## Recommended low-cost validation ladder
1. Do not interrupt current Forward and Evidence Readiness acceptance. No user action needed now.
2. Inspect upstream runtime dependencies/security/licensing and measure CPU throughput with mini/small in a separate environment **without changing BBB's production Python**, and never run unreviewed X snippets.
3. Use only past-known bitbank BTC/JPY candles and chronological timestamps. Predeclare horizons (e.g. 5m/30m/1h), cost settings and metrics before seeing results; no pretraining lookahead assumptions in its broad historic training data without checking data provenance.
4. Compare naive last-price/direction, drift/no-change, simple momentum/volatility baselines, existing Mean Reversion and Kronos on the same prospectively chosen BTC/JPY chronological periods.
5. First assess forecast error, direction hit-rate vs naive base rate, calibration and regime robustness. Then assess **out-of-sample net-after-taker-fee-and-modeled-slippage returns, DD, turnover, execution latency** of any policy generated by model. Reject on no incremental benefit.
6. Evaluate uncertainty only after explicitly exposing sampled paths rather than treating averaged `predict` result as a predictive distribution.
7. If not superior, retain results as negative evidence and do not add a continuously running LLM/model into BBB. Human must authorize any Live gate change.
