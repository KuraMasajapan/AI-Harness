## Stage 2 findings — distinctive, not duplicating field report principles

### Priority 1 — Buy/Hold spread as a cost mitigation experiment: CANDIDATE (A++ research value)
- Hoheto's 2023 note discusses changing the entry threshold and persistence/exit threshold separately to reduce turnover.
- Original primary source independently verified: Novy-Marx & Velikov, *A Taxonomy of Anomalies and Their Trading Costs*, NBER 2014 working paper / Review of Financial Studies 2016, https://www.nber.org/papers/w20721 and https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhv063.
- The paper concerns **stocks**, not BTC/JPY. Do not transfer statistical claims or numeric thresholds to bitbank.
- Novel addition to prior `2026-10-14-hoheto-execution-cost-research.md`: explicit **entry/hold/exit hysteresis** test distinct from only making entry stricter.
- Compare no-filter baseline, entry-only threshold and full hysteresis on identical time span; trade count, holding duration, missed opportunities, fees, net equity/DD, percent exposed; untouched Holdout; limited grid.

### Priority 2 — Mean-reversion signal decay and decision latency: CANDIDATE (A)
- 2019 article observed short-horizon mean reversion yet reported no backtest gain; signal predictive content decayed over seconds.
- This does **not** prove BBB's 5m champion is invalid: original is old bitFlyer FXBTC/JPY microstructure at seconds scale.
- Experiment: measure predictive lift by forward horizon on BBB's own frozen historical BTC/JPY candles; separate signal quality from execution delay and actual net profitability; do not assume a longer horizon is automatically better.

### Priority 3 — Trend / volatility no-entry filter: CANDIDATE (A)
- 2020 article A/B-tested trend-aligned quoting on bitFlyer HFT; do not transplant maker positioning or its returns.
- Test Shadow Mean Reversion vs version that suppresses entries during predeclared trend/abrupt-volatility regimes, never force liquidations without separate controls.
- Use past-only regime information and out-of-sample net returns. Regression danger: reduce losing entries while excluding most winning ones.
- VPIN case study (2019 final): author stopped an apparently promising trading strategy due to slippage/false triggers/capital inefficiency, and suggested risk-warning potential. Avoid costly VPIN ingestion unless simple volatility baselines fail.

### Priority 4 — A/B comparison and decision telemetry: CANDIDATE (A)
- 2022 failure article specifies decision/indicator/position/API latency/market context logging and a paired comparison instead of reading causality from a changed PnL curve.
- Existing BBB has Forward, Shadow, cost parity and stale detection; **reuse and extend existing run metadata** rather than build a new system.
- Explicit control-group comparison, reason-coded NO TRADE, chosen opportunity vs skipped opportunities, one-variable-at-a-time testing.

### Priority 5 — Execution/event safety: already tracked (A, no separate new feature until appropriate phase)
- 2019 inventory paper warns of position API delay and inaccurate state; maps to `2026-10-12-grok-live-trading-lessons.md` sections 7, 8, 27.
- HFT architecture paper mentions monitoring stale websocket data; maps to multi-dimensional readiness and liveness.
- Needs bitbank-specific capability and tests before Live. **Not** an argument to implement an HFT stack now.
