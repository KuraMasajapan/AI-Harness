## Surface immediately

- Major new free capability
- Important Plus limitation or allowance change
- New official connector or trigger
- Significant pricing / rate-limit change
- Useful open-source release with practical adoption
- New local model that materially lowers hardware requirements
- Repeated user reports of bugs or hidden limitations
- Real-world implementation that can simplify AI-Harness
- Evidence that an existing Harness layer can be removed or replaced
- Failure mode that should influence Rule / Access / Validation design
- Research showing a meaningful trade-off in self-improving harnesses

## Evaluation bias

Prefer:
- fewer moving parts,
- clearer boundaries,
- reversible changes,
- deterministic checks where possible,
- Human authority for consequential changes,
- model-neutral design.

Be skeptical of:
- self-modification without approval,
- agent-count inflation,
- benchmark gains with major complexity growth,
- patch-on-patch architectures,
- systems that hide failure modes.

## Usually ignore

- Minor benchmark wins without practical implications
- Rumors without corroboration
- "god mode" / "ultimate setup" claims with no operational evidence
